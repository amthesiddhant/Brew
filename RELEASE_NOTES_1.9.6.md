Brew 1.9.6 makes update alerts easier to catch.

What's new in this version:

• When a new version is available, Brew now also sends a Mac notification — so you'll know even when Brew is tucked away in the menu bar or running in the background. Clicking it brings Brew to the front, right where you can update.

• You'll still see the usual in-app update prompt when Brew is open.
