Brew 1.9.8 keeps everyone on the latest version.

What's new in this version:

• When a newer version of Brew is available, the app now asks you to update before you continue. You'll see a friendly "Update required" screen with a single "Update now" button that downloads the new version and restarts Brew for you — no manual steps.

• This makes sure everyone is always running the most up-to-date, best-working version of Brew.

• If Brew can't reach the update service, it opens as usual so you're never locked out.

Enjoy your coffee. ☕
