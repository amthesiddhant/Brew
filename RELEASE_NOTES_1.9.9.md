Brew 1.9.9 adds usage tracking.

What's new in this version:

• Brew now keeps a simple daily record of how much you've brewed — total brewing time, Slack-online time, number of sessions, and your longest session — saved once per day to your team's usage sheet.

• It happens quietly in the background using your existing connection. There's nothing to set up, and your on-device history is always the source of truth.

• If Brew can't reach the sheet, nothing is lost — it simply catches up the next time you open the app.

Enjoy your coffee. ☕
