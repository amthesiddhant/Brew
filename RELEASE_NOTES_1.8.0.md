Brew 1.8.0 keeps your Mac awake, stays online on Slack, and now updates itself — no reinstalls.

What's new in this version:

• Built-in updates. Brew now checks for new versions on its own and can update itself with one click, right from the app window. When a new version is ready, you'll see a gentle prompt — click Update and Brew restarts fresh.

• Connect once, stay current. The first time, you'll connect Brew to your Salesforce account so it can fetch updates securely. After that it just works in the background.

• A friendlier start. If Brew isn't connected yet, it opens on a simple welcome screen that walks you through connecting. The app won't get stuck on a blank or half-working window.

• Simpler and more reliable. We removed an experimental feature that didn't work for everyone, tidied things up under the hood, and made the app more dependable day to day.

To update from an older version: open Brew, connect once when asked, and it'll offer the update automatically. Enjoy your coffee. ☕
