Brew 1.9.5 adds access control.

What's new in this version:

• Brew now checks whether your account is enabled for the app when you open it. If you're on the access list, Brew opens as usual. If you're not yet enabled, you'll see a friendly screen letting you know, with a Retry button and a way to sign out.

• The check happens quietly in the background using your existing connection — there's nothing extra to set up or sign in to.
