Brew 1.10.0 makes access and usage tracking work for everyone.

What's new in this version:

• Brew now checks your access and records your daily usage through a shared, secure connection — so it works the same for every teammate, not just the person who set it up.

• The first time you open this version, Brew asks you to sign in with your Salesforce Google account once. That quick sign-in is all it needs from then on — it's remembered for next time.

• Your daily brewing summary (total time, Slack-online time, sessions, and longest session) keeps saving quietly in the background. As always, your on-device history is the source of truth, and nothing is lost if you're offline — Brew catches up next time.

Enjoy your coffee. ☕
