Brew 1.12.0 makes the admin team view interactive.

What's new in this version:

• Admins get a searchable teammate picker at the top of the Insights dashboard. Pick a name — or "All teammates" — and the whole dashboard updates to match: the stat cards, the chart, and the summary tiles, not just the table.

• "All teammates" shows the team's combined numbers at a glance. Pick a single person to see just their brewing. By default you'll still land on your own dashboard, live as always.

• Your own view stays live and up-to-the-second. Teammate and team-wide views show recorded daily history from the shared log.

• Everyone else's experience is unchanged — you see your own numbers, and only your own.
