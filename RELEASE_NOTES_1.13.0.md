Brew 1.13.0 adds a Brew timer and smarter, automatic stopping.

What's new in this version:

• Brew for a set time. Pick how long to stay awake — 15 minutes, 30 minutes, 1 hour, 2 hours, 4 hours, or 8 hours — right from the main window or the menu bar. Brew counts down and stops on its own when the time's up, so you never leave it running all night by accident.

• The timer shows the time left, ticking down to zero, with a bar that empties as you go.

• Stops itself when you close the lid. Shut your laptop and Brew steps aside so your Mac can sleep normally.

• Stops itself on low battery. If you're unplugged and the battery drops to 10%, Brew stops to help save what's left.

• You'll get a friendly notification whenever Brew stops on its own, telling you why — the timer finished, the lid closed, or the battery ran low.

Enjoy your coffee. ☕
