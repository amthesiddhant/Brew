Brew 1.11.0 brings a team view for admins.

What's new in this version:

• If you're an admin, your Insights dashboard now has a new "Team Usage" section — a clean, searchable table of everyone's daily brewing at a glance. You can filter by name or email and sort any column.

• Everyone else's dashboard is unchanged: you still see your own numbers, and only your own. Who counts as an admin is managed centrally, so nothing changes on your end.

• Behind the scenes, daily usage now saves more reliably — each day keeps a single, tidy record instead of occasionally repeating itself.

Enjoy your coffee. ☕
