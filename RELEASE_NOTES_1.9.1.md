Brew 1.9.1 makes your Insights dashboard update live and keeps your history for good.

What's new in this version:

• Live dashboard. Your Insights now update in real time — while you're brewing, the numbers and today's bar tick up second by second, with a gentle "Live" indicator so you know it's counting.

• Your history is yours to keep. Insights are stored safely on your own Mac and are never cleared — there's no reset button and nothing to lose track of. Your streak and totals just keep growing. (If you ever uninstall Brew, its insights are removed along with it.)

Enjoy your coffee. ☕
