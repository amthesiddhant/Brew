Brew 1.9.0 introduces your very own Insights dashboard — see exactly how much you've kept your Mac awake, at a glance.

What's new in this version:

• Insights dashboard. Tap the new "View Insights" button (or open it from the menu-bar icon) to see your brewing at a glance — Today, This Week, This Month, and All Time — with a clean bar chart of your recent days and months.

• The numbers that matter. See your current day streak, your longest single session, your average session, how much time you've spent showing as online on Slack, your total number of sessions, and the date you first started brewing.

• Live and private. The chart updates as you brew, and all of it stays on your own Mac — nothing is uploaded anywhere. You can clear your history anytime with one tap.

Note: insights start counting from the moment you install this version, so your dashboard will fill in as you use Brew from here on.

Enjoy your coffee. ☕
